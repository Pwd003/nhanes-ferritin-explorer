Ferritin Explorer

Identification of low ferritin (≤30 ng/mL) in nonpregnant women aged 20–44 years with haemoglobin ≥12 g/dL

RESEARCH USE ONLY · Not a clinical diagnostic tool. This browser-based tool reproduces the study’s frozen equations, not a clinical diagnosis or testing recommendation. A low estimate cannot rule out iron deficiency or justify omitting a clinically indicated ferritin test. Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser; the page does not store or transmit them.

1. Synthetic profile

Enter hypothetical values in the units shown. Age, Hb, MCV and RDW must be within the original development range. BMI and race/ethnicity may be left blank. If race/ethnicity is unspecified, the two race-dependent models are not calculated; NO_RACE requires BMI, while CBC_ONLY does not. Age, Hb, MCV and RDW remain required for the common hypothetical profile.

The limits are observed development-sample ranges, not clinical reference ranges. Race categories follow RIDRETH3 and are not biological classifications.

2. Compare model estimates

Model-estimated probability of concurrent ferritin ≤30 ng/mL, not future risk. Same hypothetical profile, four separately fitted equations.

Load a fictional example or enter values to compare the equations.

Differences in these individual estimates are not changes in diagnostic accuracy or clinical net benefit. Simpler equations use their own development-period coefficients. The study compared these models in the same complete-case validation sample; no missing inputs are imputed.

5. Sensitivity analyses and model specification

Inflammation restriction

hsCRP ≤5 mg/L: 598 women, 170 low-ferritin cases. A1 AUC 0.7393 (95% CI 0.6784–0.8003); observed sensitivity under the original fixed rule 93.40%.

Alternative reference outcome

Ferritin <15 ng/mL: 45 of 825 women. Original A1 ranking AUC 0.8312 (95% CI 0.7591–0.9034). This does not represent calibrated predictions for the alternative outcome.

Complete equation coefficients and coding

All equations use p = 1 / (1 + exp(−η)), where η is the intercept plus the sum of coefficient × input. Race indicators equal 1 only for the selected category; category 1 is the reference. No fitting or recalibration occurs in this tool.
